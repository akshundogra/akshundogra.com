🧩 GTM Engineering / Agentic Systems

Building an Autonomous GTM Agent Network ("Circuit")

The Problem

SEO/GEO, performance marketing, content creation, and sales handovers operated in silos. Cross-checking analytics, capturing buyer intent, and spotting ad spend leaks ate hours weekly — leaving high-intent leads unrouted and budget leaks unnoticed.

The Solution
  • Architected "Circuit" — a multi-agent GTM stack with specialized SEO/GEO, Performance Marketing, Executor, and AirOps Content agents
  • Engineered an intent-scoring & account enrichment engine capturing GA4, GSC, Sistrix, and paid ad telemetry to enrich buyer profiles in HubSpot CRM
  • Automated Sales Handover: routed high-intent buyer signals directly to sales reps via real-time Slack alerts and automated deal/task creation
  • Enforced strict human-in-the-loop (HITL) review gates and verification rules before any ad spend adjustment or external outreach
Key Insight

"Single automation scripts are brittle shortcuts. A multi-agent network that scores buyer intent, bridges marketing signals directly to sales reps, and enforces human verification is what drives revenue at scale."

🗂️ GTM Engineering / Productivity Systems

Chief-of-Staff & Executor Agents

The Problem

Running multi-agent systems and GTM operations simultaneously created context-switching drag — open tasks, meeting prep, and operational reviews slipped between the cracks.

The Solution
  • Built Daily & Weekly Coworker agents (Chief-of-Staff) running automated triage sweeps across Slack, Teams, and Notion Task Trackers
  • Engineered an Executor agent that scans open tasks, evaluates feasibility via a capabilities registry (`capabilities.md`), and drafts deliverables before meetings
  • Automated Monday Week Plans & Friday Reviews with Notion Run Log reconciliation and Slack notification loops
  • Enforced a strict semi-autonomous model: agents draft and stage work; human approves and ships
Key Insight

"The highest-leverage internal agents don't replace decision-making — they eliminate pre-meeting prep time by delivering 80% drafted work straight to your review queue."

⚡ Marketing Automation

Turning Anonymous Website Visitors into Qualified Pipeline

The Problem

Visitors hitting MOFU/BOFU pages — pricing, solutions, case studies — and leaving without a trace. No contact info, no form fill, no signal to act on. High-intent prospects were disappearing silently.

The Solution
  • Used RB2B + Apollo to de-anonymize website visitors and extract contact details
  • Sent data via API webhook through Make to Clay for ICP scoring
  • Clay segmented leads: High Value → straight to Lemlist outreach, Low Value → discarded
  • Mid-value leads triggered a Slack confirmation — SDR reviews and accepts/rejects manually
  • Decision sent back to Clay → Lemlist or discarded, closing the human-in-the-loop
Key Insight

"AI can hallucinate and make bad decisions at scale. Building a human checkpoint for edge cases isn't a weakness — it's what keeps the system trustworthy and the SDRs from burning good leads."

🌐 Web Dev + AI Automation

Personalized ABM Landing Pages at Scale with AI

The Problem

Cold ABM targets and "not now" leads sitting stagnant in the CRM. Manual personalization took too long — creating custom content per account wasn't scalable for a small team.

The Solution
  • Pulled cold/stagnant leads from CRM → Make → Clay for enrichment
  • Clay + ChatGPT generated personalized content per account (company logo, custom message, personalized hero, industry-specific copy)
  • Built a dynamic Webflow template — 60% static, 40% auto-populated fields
  • SDRs added custom sandbox demos or videos (via tools like NanoBanana)
  • Pages distributed via outreach campaigns, LinkedIn thought leadership (CEO/CTO), and company-based remarketing
Key Insight

"ABM used to mean weeks of manual work per account. With the right pipeline, you can create genuinely personalized experiences in minutes — without sacrificing quality."

🔍 SEO + LLM Visibility

Making a Small SaaS Visible in the Age of AI Search

The Problem

A small SaaS with limited domain authority — invisible on LLM-based search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Bing AI). Traditional SEO wasn't enough. Needed a GEO-first strategy before competitors caught on.

The Solution
  • Built a dynamic schema generator per page — structured for Google's 2MB crawl limit and LLM readability
  • Used Peec AI + Bing Webmaster to track which prompts ranked — on-page fixes, FAQs, and schema for weak prompts
  • Identified relevant LinkedIn and Reddit communities — answered queries to build LLM citation signals
  • Optimized G2 and Crunchbase profiles as trusted LLM reference sources (high-authority third-party signals)
Key Insight

"LLMs cite structured, opinionated, community-validated content. Your G2 profile and a Reddit answer can outrank your blog post in ChatGPT — if you treat them as part of your SEO strategy."

📈 Performance Marketing

Building a Full-Funnel B2B Paid Media Engine

The Problem

Paid media was completely unstructured — Google spend scattered across generic keywords with near-zero pipeline, LinkedIn tested sporadically with no ICP targeting, and no KPI framework linking spend to demos or revenue.

LinkedIn — Demand Creation
  • Boosted founder-led thought leadership directly to curated ICP lists — achieving 6x industry benchmark CTR
  • Built custom company and persona lists replacing native targeting — 35% CTR increase
  • Killed gated content and lead gen forms — weak quality, no pipeline
  • Evolved into 1:1 ABM campaigns for more than 50 named accounts
Google Ads — Demand Capture
  • Pivoted from broad keyword clusters to competitor search only — users already in buying mode
  • Reduced generic campaigns to exact-match with strict spend caps and kill rules
  • Added display retargeting as second conversion layer
  • Established kill criteria before launch — cut underperforming channels fast
Key Insight

"Paid media in B2B is not a volume play. LinkedIn creates qualified demand inside the exact ICP, Google Search harvests it, and retargeting converts it. Every euro must link to pipeline."
